Our Story

Who We Are

Welcome to Highway Stops Retail Ltd (HSRL), a trusted family-owned fuel and convenience retailer. Our network of modern service stations brings together premium fuel, quality refreshments and comfortable facilities, all designed to make every journey smoother, faster and more enjoyable.

Highway Stops Retail Ltd (HSRL) was launched in December 2011 by Sharad Raja. Prior to HSRL, Sharad founded the UK’s largest independent forecourt operator Motor Fuel Group (MFG), his drive, vision and values remain core to the principals we hold today.

Highway Stops is led by an ambitious, forward-thinking leadership team with decades of combined experience in the forecourt and convenience retail sector. Building on the strong foundations laid by our founder, Sharad Raja, the team combines deep industry knowledge with a clear vision for the future. They drive the business forward through strategic growth, innovation and a commitment to operational excellence.

HSRL has followed a proven growth strategy, built on disciplined investment, operational efficiency and a clear focus on site-by-site performance. With the continued support of our investors and financial partners, we are actively seeking to expand our network by acquiring high-quality forecourt assets. We look for well-located sites with strong potential, where our experience in forecourt operations, convenience retail and site development can add long-term value.

Our Values

What Matters to Us

01

Customers Commitment

Our customers are at the heart of everything we do. We are committed to providing a consistently high standard of service designed around our customers’ needs at an affordable price. Our aim is simple: to make every visit welcoming and efficient.

02

Community Engagement

We recognise the important role our forecourts play in the communities they serve. HSRL provide vital infrastructure and essential services that supports local communities. We are proud to create local employment and support initiatives that make a positive difference in the areas where we operate.

03

Sustainability

As the transport landscape evolves, we are committed to supporting the transition to a low-carbon future. We are investing in an ultra-rapid electric vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure across our network and have installed solar photovoltaic (PV) at our sites to reduce our environmental impact. By embracing new technologies, we aim to meet the needs of every driver today while preparing our business for tomorrow.

Our People

Leadership Team

Shilan Raja

Managing Director

Shilan grew up in a family business at the heart of the UK forecourt sector, which gave him a deep understanding of the industry from an early age. After graduating from university, he spent ten years at a leading accounting software company in the City of London, gaining valuable experience in technology, finance and commercial strategy.

Shilan now leads Highway Stops Retail Ltd, bringing a modern, data-driven perspective to the business while staying true to the values and vision established by his father and founder, Sharad Raja.

Tony Head

Director

Tony has spent almost 40 years in petrol retailing. His experience covers retail management, forecourt development, and property acquisitions / disposals, including managing multiple raze-and-rebuild projects from planning through to reopening. He began his career with Norfolk House and Repsol Petroleum before spending 15 years at BP Oil, where he held roles in retail management, development, project management, and acquisitions / disposals, including new-to-industry (NTI) sites. He then left the corporate world to join the original MFG, where he stayed until the business was sold in 2011. Tony has been with Highway Stops since the beginning and has played a key part in its growth, helping to build the network and the company. His knowledge of both the operational and property sides of the industry means he understands what makes a forecourt work, for customers, staff and the business alike.
