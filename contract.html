Contract Management

Become a Contract Manager

Run your own business, backed by HSRL, an established forecourt operator.

HSRL give driven entrepreneurs the opportunity to lead & grow in the fast-paced world of UK roadside retail.

At HSRL, our contract managers are at the heart of our success. We are looking for motivated, customer-focused people to run our forecourt sites and grow with us as we expand our network.

What is a Contract Manager?

As a contract manager, you take responsibility for the day-to-day running of a Highway Stops forecourt and convenience store. You lead your own team, deliver excellent customer service and drive the performance of your site, all with the support, systems and investment of an established forecourt business.

It’s an opportunity to run your own business without the cost and risk of buying a site.

Why Partner with Us?

  • Established network: Operate a forecourt under trusted fuel brands such as BP, Esso and Shell.
  • No property investment: We own and invest in the sites, so you can focus on running the business.
  • Training & support: Ongoing guidance from our experienced operations team, plus training, systems and supplier relationships.
  • Earning potential: Your income is directly linked to the success of your site.
  • Family values: A family-run business that values long-term partnerships and treats its people with respect.
  • Room to grow: As our network expands, high-performing contract managers may have the chance to run more sites.

Your Responsibilities

  • Managing the daily operation of the forecourt, shop and valeting facilities.
  • Recruiting, training and leading your site team.
  • Delivering high standards of customer service, safety and compliance.
  • Maintaining stock control, store standards and promotional activity.
  • Driving sales and profitability across fuel, retail and food to go.

Who We’re Looking For

  • Experience in retail, convenience, hospitality or forecourt management.
  • Strong leadership and people management skills.
  • A hands-on, customer-first approach.
  • Commercial awareness and a drive to grow sales.
  • Good organisational skills and an eye for detail.
  • A commitment to safety, compliance and high standards.

Previous forecourt experience is an advantage but not essential. Full training is provided.

How do I Become a Contract Manager?

  1. Complete an online Application Form (Apply Now box).
  2. Be invited and attend an interview at HSRL Head Office.
  3. Review opportunities within your chosen location.
  4. Accept site offer and sign contracts.
  5. Attend the HSRL training programme.
  6. Take over site location.
